Find Y Crew
BEFORE YOU START

Share your location with your crew?

Find Y Crew only works if you choose to share where you are. Here's exactly what that means.

What we share

Your live position, your name, avatar and any status you post.

Who sees it

Only people in your crew. Never the festival, advertisers or other attendees as an identifiable person.

For how long

Only while the app is open. Your position is deleted when you leave, and automatically after 30 minutes without updates.

You're in control

Go invisible or switch to approximate location (~100 m) at any time from the Crew tab.

Anonymous crowd statistics (optional)

If you allow it, your position is counted, without your name or code, in heatmaps and crowd levels per area that the festival uses to improve the site. Groups smaller than a minimum size are never shown.
